Preserving Your Memories

Scrapbook albums have exitsted since the beginning of photographs.However, the sad fact is that photographs and most printed material are not permanent:they will fade and yellow with age.Scrapbookers of today are aware of these problems,and the industry is providingremedies to minimize deterioration.For the best results,avoid using material with high acid content which can cause photos and paper to deteriorate.Another thing to avoid is lignin, a materual that is the bonding element in wood fibers.Over time,paper with ligin will becom yellow and brittle, so yoy should only use ligin-free products.


Your albums should contain page protectors to shield the pages from smudges,oil,and dirt that can be transferred from your hands.You should never use albums with sticky "magnetic" pages.The sticky substance will be tranferred to the photo and backing paper causing deterioration.Never crop Plaroid photos:they will curl and fall apart. Mount all memorabilia on acid-free cardstock paper,and photocopy all newspapers clipping on acid-free paper.
